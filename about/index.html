About Us

Heating & Cooling Done Right, From the Detroit River to Lake Erie

Windsor Furnace Repair is a family-run HVAC shop on Ouellette Avenue serving homeowners across Windsor and Essex County. We have spent years keeping the border city warm through deep January cold snaps and cool through sticky Lake Erie summers, one honest service call at a time.

Our Story

A Windsor Story, Built One Service Call at a Time

Windsor Furnace Repair started as a small, family-run shop on Ouellette Avenue with a simple promise to neighbours across the border city: show up on time, diagnose honestly, and fix it right the first time. Decades of southwestern Ontario winters and humid Lake Erie summers later, that promise still drives every service call we run.

We live where you live, in the brick century homes of Walkerville and Sandwich, the post-war bungalows of Riverside and Forest Glade, and the growing subdivisions of LaSalle, Tecumseh, and Lakeshore. That local knowledge matters. We know which furnace models were common in 1970s builds across East Windsor, how the lake-effect wind off Lake Erie punishes outdoor condensers in Amherstburg and Leamington, and why a humid August pushes older central-air systems in South Windsor to their limit.

Every job we take, whether it is an emergency furnace repair on a freezing January night, a ductless mini-split install in a Walkerville attic conversion, or a heat pump upgrade that lowers a family's year-round energy bills, gets the same careful attention. Our licensed technicians service every major brand, walk you through the diagnosis in plain language, and back their work with straightforward pricing and a satisfaction guarantee.

As Windsor has grown and changed, so have we, adding duct cleaning, smart thermostat installation, and high-efficiency heat pumps to keep pace with how Essex County families heat and cool their homes today. The foundation has not moved: honest work, fair quotes, and a real person picking up the phone when you call (548) 708-8221.
